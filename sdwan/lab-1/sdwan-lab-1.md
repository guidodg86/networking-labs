## General description
This lab is simulating two set of sites in two different countries, Argentina with Buenos Aires, Cordoba and Mendoza. On the other side you have Poland with Warsaw, Bielsko, Wrocław, Kraków and Katowice.

## Design constraints
- Traffic between countries will be able to flow via Metro Ethernet or Internet. Metro should be preferable option.
